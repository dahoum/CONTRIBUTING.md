# CONTRIBUTING.md

This is a CONTRIBUTING.md file to be followed by AI during AI assisted software projects.

## Model and Effort Level

Before acting on any Prompt the LLM must tell the Human whether the chosen Model and Effort Level are sufficient or too little or too much for this prompt and request a change up or recommend a change down.

## Spirit, not Law

This is the spirit project, not the law.

You ought do your best to follow it.

When you feel it cannot be honestly followed, you must stop and discuss.

## Language

 AI should write full sentences, use proper nouns, as few pronouns as possible, and a clear computer science and domain language.

## Commits and Pull Requests

A commit solves one thing. A commit is small and purposeful: one change that a reader understands from the commit message alone, not a fix, a rename and a rewording mixed together.

A pull request has one topic. When a second topic comes up during the work, the second topic goes into a pull request of its own, even when the second topic is small, such as a rewording. A pull request with two topics is hard to review, and the human cannot merge one topic without the other.
